# Curated Resource Library

Every link is free unless marked **(paid)**. Regulatory and exchange sites are **primary sources** —
prefer them over any blog, video, or course when the two disagree.

---

## 1. Regulators and official bodies (always check here first)

| Resource | Link | Why it's useful |
|---|---|---|
| **SEBI** | https://www.sebi.gov.in/ | The regulator. Circulars, orders, rules, and the intermediary registry. When a rule matters, read the source. |
| **SEBI Investor Website** | https://investor.sebi.gov.in/ | Plain-language investor education, cautionary messages, and the "do's and don'ts". Zero sales agenda. |
| **SEBI — Intermediary search** | https://www.sebi.gov.in/intermediaries.html | Verify that a broker, adviser, or research analyst is actually registered. Use before dealing with anyone. |
| **SEBI SCORES** | https://scores.sebi.gov.in/ | File and track grievances against market intermediaries. |
| **RBI** | https://www.rbi.org.in/ | Monetary policy, repo rate, inflation data, financial stability reports. |
| **AMFI** | https://www.amfiindia.com/ | Mutual fund industry body: NAV data, scheme categorisation, investor awareness. |
| **MoSPI** | https://mospi.gov.in/ | Official CPI/inflation and GDP releases. |
| **Income Tax Department** | https://www.incometax.gov.in/ | Authoritative on capital gains taxation and reporting. |
| **Investor Charter / ODR portal** | https://smartodr.in/ | Online dispute resolution for securities market disputes. |

## 5. Books

### Start here
| Book | Author | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Let's Talk Money* | Monika Halan | Indian personal finance, beginner-friendly, no jargon, no product pushing. |
| *The Psychology of Money* | Morgan Housel | Short, readable, and about the behaviour that actually determines outcomes. |
| *The Little Book of Common Sense Investing* | John Bogle | The clearest case for low-cost index investing. |

### Fundamentals & investing
| Book | Author | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *The Interpretation of Financial Statements* | Benjamin Graham | ~120 pages, timeless, exactly what a beginner needs. |
| *The Intelligent Investor* | Benjamin Graham | Read Ch. 8 and 20 first. The margin-of-safety idea is the whole book. |
| *Coffee Can Investing* | Saurabh Mukherjea | Indian long-term equity framework. |
| *Diamonds in the Dust* | Saurabh Mukherjea | Indian quality-screening approach with concrete filters. |
| *Financial Shenanigans* | Howard Schilit | How accounting manipulation actually looks in statements. Sobering and practical. |
| *One Up on Wall Street* | Peter Lynch | Accessible thinking about businesses you can observe. |

### Trading & psychology
| Book | Author | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Trading in the Zone* | Mark Douglas | Probability thinking; the standard text on trading psychology. |
| *Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets* | John Murphy | The standard TA reference. |
| *Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques* | Steve Nison | The original source on candlesticks. |
| *Fooled by Randomness* | Nassim Taleb | Why small samples can't distinguish skill from luck. |
| *Thinking, Fast and Slow* | Daniel Kahneman | The science behind every bias in Week 5. Dense but foundational. |
| *Market Wizards* | Jack Schwager | Interviews with traders; note how much they talk about risk vs entries. |

### Indian market history (worth reading once)
| Book | Author | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *The Scam* | Debashis Basu & Sucheta Dalal | The Harshad Mehta scam and how Indian markets were manipulated. Essential context. |
| *Bulls, Bears and Other Beasts* | Santosh Nair | A narrative history of the Indian market. |

### Advanced (bookmark for later, not now)
- *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* — John Hull
- *Option Volatility and Pricing* — Sheldon Natenberg
- *Valuation* — McKinsey / Koller et al.

### ❌ What to avoid, specifically
- Any channel or group offering "calls", "targets", "sure-shot tips", or a paid Telegram/WhatsApp group.
- "Earn ₹X per day from options" content — this is the most heavily monetised and least honest niche.
- Anyone showing P&L screenshots as proof of skill. Screenshots are trivially faked and prove nothing about process.
- Anyone offering guaranteed or assured returns. That's a fraud signature, not a strategy.
- Courses **(paid)** costing tens of thousands promising trading income. Everything a beginner needs is free above.
