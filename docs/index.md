# Indian Stock Market — 6-Week Beginner Roadmap

A structured, self-paced learning path for someone starting with **zero knowledge** of the Indian
stock market. Designed for **2–3 hours every weekend** over **6 weeks**.

!!! quote "The goal"
    Build a strong foundation — **not** to make quick money.

!!! danger "Educational content only"
    This site is **not** investment advice, not a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and
    makes **no promise of returns**. No specific stock is recommended anywhere.
    **Do not invest real money during these 6 weeks.**
    Read the full [disclaimer](disclaimer.md).

## The 6-week roadmap

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-numeric-1-circle:{ .lg .middle } **Stock Market Fundamentals**

    ---

    Shares, NSE/BSE, Nifty & Sensex, IPOs, demat accounts, order types, market cap, liquidity,
    settlement and corporate actions.

    :material-clock-outline: 2–3 h

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Start Week 1](week-01-stock-market-fundamentals.md)

-   :material-numeric-2-circle:{ .lg .middle } **Investing Fundamentals**

    ---

    Compounding, inflation and real return, risk vs horizon, diversification, asset allocation,
    index funds, NAV, expense ratio, SIPs and dividends.

    :material-clock-outline: 2–3 h

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Start Week 2](week-02-investing-fundamentals.md)

-   :material-numeric-3-circle:{ .lg .middle } **Fundamental Analysis**

    ---

    P&L, balance sheet and cash flow; EBITDA, EPS, P/E, P/B, ROE, ROCE, debt/equity and margins;
    reading an annual report.

    :material-clock-outline: 3 h

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Start Week 3](week-03-fundamental-analysis.md)

-   :material-numeric-4-circle:{ .lg .middle } **Trading & Technical Analysis**

    ---

    Candlesticks and OHLC, trends, support and resistance, breakouts, volume, moving averages,
    RSI, MACD, stop losses and risk/reward.

    :material-clock-outline: 3 h

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Start Week 4](week-04-trading-technical-analysis.md)

-   :material-numeric-5-circle:{ .lg .middle } **Risk Management & Psychology**

    ---

    Position sizing, risk per trade, drawdown maths, leverage, portfolio heat, FOMO, revenge
    trading and overtrading. **The most important week.**

    :material-clock-outline: 2–3 h

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Start Week 5](week-05-risk-management-psychology.md)

-   :material-numeric-6-circle:{ .lg .middle } **Futures & Options — Intro Only**

    ---

    Futures, expiry, lot size, margin; calls and puts, strikes, premium, ITM/ATM/OTM, intrinsic
    vs time value and the Greeks. **Vocabulary only — no trading.**

    :material-clock-outline: 2–3 h

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Start Week 6](week-06-futures-and-options-intro.md)

</div>

## Ground rules for these 6 weeks

!!! failure "Don't"
    - No real money. Paper trading and mock portfolios only.
    - No F&O trading. Week 6 is *vocabulary only*.
    - No tips from Telegram, WhatsApp groups, or YouTube "calls".
    - No leverage, margin, or MTF.

!!! success "Do"
    - Verify every claim against a primary source — SEBI, NSE, BSE, AMFI, RBI, or the company's own filings.
    - Write things down. It beats watching more videos.
    - Judge your decisions by **process**, not by outcome.

!!! warning "Rules change"
    Links were correct at the time of writing. Settlement cycles, margin rules, lot sizes and
    taxation in India change regularly — always confirm current rules on the
    [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/), [NSE](https://www.nseindia.com/),
    [BSE](https://www.bseindia.com/), [AMFI](https://www.amfiindia.com/) or
    [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/) site.
