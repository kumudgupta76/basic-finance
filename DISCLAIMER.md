# Disclaimer

## Educational content only

Everything in this repository is **general educational material** about how the Indian
securities market works. It is **not**:

- personalised investment advice,
- a research report,
- a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security,
- a promise, projection, or guarantee of any return.

## No stock recommendations

Where a company or index is named, it is used **only as a publicly available example** for
learning how to read data — never as a suggestion to invest in it.

## Markets involve risk of loss

Equity, derivatives, and mutual fund investments are subject to market risk. You can lose
part or all of your capital. Derivatives (futures and options) can cause losses **larger than
the amount you put in**, because of leverage.

SEBI's own published studies on individual traders in the equity derivatives segment have
repeatedly found that the large majority of individual F&O traders make net losses.
Take that seriously before ever going near that segment.

## Do not use real money during the learning period

The 6-week roadmap is designed to be completed with **zero rupees at risk**. Paper trading,
mock portfolios, and observation only.

## Rules change

Settlement cycles, margin requirements, lot sizes, taxation, and disclosure norms in India
change over time. Always verify current rules from primary sources:

- SEBI — https://www.sebi.gov.in/
- SEBI Investor Website — https://investor.sebi.gov.in/
- NSE — https://www.nseindia.com/
- BSE — https://www.bseindia.com/
- AMFI — https://www.amfiindia.com/
- RBI — https://www.rbi.org.in/

## Before you ever transact

- Deal only with a **SEBI-registered** intermediary. Verify at
  https://www.sebi.gov.in/intermediaries.html
- For advice, use a **SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (RIA)** or a Registered Research
  Analyst — and ask for their registration number.
- Report grievances via **SEBI SCORES** — https://scores.sebi.gov.in/
- Ignore anyone guaranteeing returns. Guaranteed returns in equities are a red flag for fraud.
